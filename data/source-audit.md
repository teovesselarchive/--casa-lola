## Design implications

The real room is far more expressive than the current stacked WordPress presentation. Let the photographs contribute the multiple colors while choosing one intentional interface accent (magenta or warm terracotta could be coherent). Woven light, mural scale, warm natural surfaces and generous convivial tables are stronger cues than cliché Mexican clip-art. Build a clear visit/reserve path, then food, recurring gatherings and private celebrations. Retain recognizable logo, no invented emblem.
